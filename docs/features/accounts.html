Agentstrap Docs

Roblox accounts

Save, switch and launch Roblox with a selected account.

Agentstrap can save multiple Roblox accounts locally and use the selected one when launching Roblox.

Adding an account

Open Settings → Accounts and use Quick Sign In. Agentstrap validates the session against Roblox and stores only the account profile locally. Credentials are protected with Windows DPAPI and are never written to logs.

Logout

Use the Logout button to sign out of the active account. The saved account list remains available for switching.

Switching

Select an account and press Switch. Only one account is active at a time. Roblox launches use the active account.

Security. Credentials are stored locally with DPAPI and are never sent anywhere except Roblox during validation.
